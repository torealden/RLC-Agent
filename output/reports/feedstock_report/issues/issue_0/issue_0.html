The Signal

[DRAFT — replace before publish] Waste and animal-fat feedstocks held a firm floor into the August 3 close while the vegetable-oil complex did the work. Distillers corn oil gave back about 3.6% on the week at Iowa plants, narrowing its premium to bleachable tallow, and the spread structure across the fats complex continues to price carbon intensity ahead of calories. The signal to watch: cash fats are not confirming the softness in board oil.
